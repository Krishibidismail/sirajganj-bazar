## 🏢 About The Project
Sirajganj Bazar is a trusted e-commerce marketplace in Bangladesh offering groceries, fashion, electronics, fresh food, and daily essentials with secure payment and fast delivery.

- **Sub-Organizations:** Tati Bazar, Gheewala Brand
- **My Role:** Founder & Lead Full Stack Developer

### 🎯 Project Vision & Goals
- Build a scalable online marketplace for local and regional products.
- Support both retail and wholesale commerce through a modern digital platform.
- Establish a strong digital presence with a long-term vision for expanding digital commerce.

### 👨‍💻 Technical Responsibilities
- Full e-commerce website design and development.
- Custom UI/UX optimized for both mobile and desktop users.
- Product category, inventory, and listing structure setup.
- Shopping cart, checkout integration, and secure payment gateway implementation.
- On-page SEO optimization and performance enhancement aligned with modern web standards.

### 🏆 Key Results
- Successfully built and launched a full-scale e-commerce platform.
- Optimized UI/UX ensuring fast performance and seamless shopping across devices.
- Built a strong digital footprint and increased online accessibility for local sellers and buyers.
